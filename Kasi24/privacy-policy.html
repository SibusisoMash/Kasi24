1. Who we are

Kasi24 ("Kasi24", "we", "us", "our") operates the website kasi24.co.za, a platform that connects landlords in [Kwamhlanga / KwaNdebele region] with tenants looking for rooms, back rooms, flats and houses to rent. Kasi24 is operated by [your full name or registered business name], of [physical address].

Under POPIA, Kasi24 is the "responsible party" for the personal information described in this policy. Our designated Information Officer is [name], contactable at [email/phone]. POPIA requires every responsible party to register its Information Officer with the Information Regulator before processing personal information — see section 15 for how to do this if you haven't yet.

2. What personal information we collect

From landlordsFull name, phone number, WhatsApp number, email address, South African ID number, login credentials, property listing details, listing photos, bank/payout details (once payments go live).
From tenantsPhone number used for WhatsApp contact (where provided), unlock/payment history, listings saved or viewed, device/browser information, IP address.
AutomaticallyUsage data (pages visited, search filters used, time on site), device type, browser type, general location inferred from IP address.
Payment dataHandled by our payment processor (see section 5) — we do not store your full card details. We do store a record that a payment was made, its amount, and its status.

3. Why we collect it

  • To create and manage landlord accounts and listings
  • To let tenants search, filter and unlock listings
  • To process the R10 unlock payment and confirm it went through
  • To contact landlords about their listings (approval, expiry reminders, unlock notifications)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, fake listings and abuse of the platform
  • To improve the site and understand how it's used
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax and consumer protection law

4. Our legal basis for processing

We only process your personal information where at least one of the following applies:

  • Consent — for example, when you create an account or submit a listing, you consent to us processing the information you provide.
  • Performance of a contract — processing needed to provide the service you've asked for, such as confirming a payment or displaying your listing.
  • Legitimate interest — such as detecting fraudulent listings or improving the platform, balanced against your right to privacy.
  • Legal obligation — such as retaining payment records for tax purposes.

5. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where necessary to run the platform:

  • Supabase (database, authentication and file storage provider) — hosts all the information described in section 2.
  • Yoco — processes the R10 unlock payment. We share only the information needed to process the transaction; we never see or store your full card details.
  • Netlify (hosting provider) — serves the website itself.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — only where we are legally required to, such as in response to a valid court order.

We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not share landlord contact details with tenants until a valid unlock payment has been confirmed.

7. Special note on ID numbers

We currently ask landlords for their South African ID number as part of our manual verification process, to reduce the risk of fake listings. Because an ID number is sensitive personal information, we are working to either encrypt this field at rest or replace it with a verified checkbox once our internal verification process confirms your identity, rather than storing the number itself long-term. If you have concerns about how your ID number is stored, contact us using the details in section 15.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and listing information — for as long as your account is active, plus [X] months after deletion, to prevent fraud and resolve disputes.
  • Payment/unlock records — as required by South African tax law (currently five years).
  • Deleted listings — removed from public view immediately; underlying records may be retained briefly for dispute resolution before permanent deletion.

9. How we protect it

We use Supabase's Row Level Security to restrict access to personal information, and our admin panel is protected against public access. We are aware that our current admin authentication check happens in the browser rather than being enforced at the database level, and we are working to strengthen this. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk.

10. Cookies and local storage

Kasi24 uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember listings you've saved and numbers you've already unlocked, so you don't have to pay twice. This information stays on your device and is lost if you clear your browser or switch devices. We do not currently use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

11. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Be told what personal information of yours we hold, and why
  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or update inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete or de-identify information we no longer have a lawful basis to keep
  • Object to us processing your information for direct marketing purposes
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where our processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (see section 15) if you believe we have handled your information unlawfully

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 15. We will respond within a reasonable time and in line with POPIA's requirements.

12. Children's information

Kasi24 is intended for users 18 and older, as listings and rental agreements involve legal and financial responsibility. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

13. If something goes wrong

If we become aware of a security compromise that has led to unauthorised access to, or acquisition of, personal information, we will notify the Information Regulator and affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible, as required by POPIA.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
