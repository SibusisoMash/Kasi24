What stays private here (by design)
  • Phone / WhatsApp / email / SA ID are never readable by strangers. Listings expose only area, rent and photos.
  • Tenant sees your number only after paying R20 and admin confirms, via get_landlord_contact() — never via a table select.
  • We bind everything to your login ID (auth.uid()), not your phone number, so knowing your number buys an attacker nothing.
  • SA ID is validated, stored once, never displayed back in full, and slated for redaction after verification (see Privacy Policy §7).
