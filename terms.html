5. R20 unlock terms and refunds

  • Price: R20 once-off per listing. The unlocked number is yours with no expiry — local storage remembers it so you don't pay twice on the same device.
  • What you get: the landlord's phone and WhatsApp number for that listing only, after payment is confirmed (`complete`). You do not get exclusivity, a viewing slot, or a held room.
  • Use your cell as reference on the Yoco payment page so we can match your payment.
  • No refund for the wrong listing: if you unlock a listing and then decide you don't like the area, price, or house rules, the R20 is not refundable — browse carefully before paying.
  • When we will help: if the listing was fake, removed for fraud, or already taken at unlock time and you report it within 48 hours with proof, we will at our discretion replace the unlock (free unlock on another listing) or re-credit you. Manual Yoco payments are confirmed by the owner in the admin panel — keep the page open until it shows `complete`, and WhatsApp us at 081 352 8749 if it stalls.
  • Chargebacks/fraudulent payments lead to account suspension.

6. Viewing safety

  • Always view the property in person before paying any deposit or signing anything.
  • View in daylight, bring someone you trust, and never pay cash to a stranger or to someone you haven't met.
  • Never share your ID, bank details, or OTPs with another user. Kasi24 will never ask for your password or OTP.
  • If something feels wrong, leave. Report the listing from the listing page.
